# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ContextLease a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy, credentials, prompt data, and security disclosures.
- Make space for contributors with different backgrounds and experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, impersonation, or bad-faith security reports.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents ContextLease in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the lead maintainer using the contact
method published on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue
containing personal or sensitive information.

Maintainers will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest,
and take action proportionate to the impact and context. Actions may include a
private warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent removal
from project spaces.
